02 · who built this room

The House You Never Chose

Imagine you hired an architect to design your home when you were eight years old.

They built exactly what an eight-year-old needed: small spaces, simple systems, everything designed around keeping that child safe, loved, and approved of by producing. Now you’re here. Running a company. Leading a team. Building something real. And you’re still living in that eight-year-old’s house.

No wonder it feels cramped. No wonder the ceiling keeps showing up no matter how hard you push. Drifting is what happens when you keep executing an ancient operating system, built by invisible architects, long after it stopped fitting the life you’re actually living.

Coasting is just a slow-motion collapse. You cannot coast your way to a true, aligned empire. You have to slow down, wake up, and see the room clearly for the first time.
