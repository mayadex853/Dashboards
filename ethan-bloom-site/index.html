    The story
    After losing his mother, thirteen-year-old Ethan finds solace in the Catholic church, a refuge that feels like home, even though he was born into a Jewish family. When his father insists on a bar mitzvah and Ethan’s secret unravels at his girlfriend’s confirmation party, the young seeker must confront the lies he’s told and the grief he’s been running from. Through the wisdom of an unconventional rabbi, the love of his complicated family, and his first romance with a cowboy-boot-wearing girl who can put him in a headlock, Ethan discovers that faith isn’t about choosing sides. It’s about honoring all the messy, beautiful pieces of who we are.

    Directed by Herschel Faber · Written by Maylen Dominguez
    Set in Miami’s Coconut Grove · Distributed in North America by Menemsha Films

    What it’s about

    Four things Ethan learns

    The Essence of Faith

    Faith is a guiding light in Ethan's journey, offering solace and strength amidst life's trials and tribulations. It is a core theme.

    The Bonds of Family

    Family provides Ethan with unwavering support and love, shaping his values and influencing his decisions throughout the film's narrative.

    Navigating Loss and Grief

    The film explores the profound impact of loss on Ethan's life, highlighting his resilience and ability to find hope amidst sorrow.

    The Sweetness of First Love

    First love blossoms in Ethan's life, bringing joy and challenges as he navigates the complexities of relationships and self-discovery.

    Meet the cast

    The Bloomfields & company

    Discover the talented actors who brought the characters of Ethan Bloom to life.

    as Gary Bloomfield

    Joshua Malina

    Joshua Malina is a celebrated actor known for his iconic roles in “The West Wing” and “Scandal.” His extensive television credits include “The Big Bang Theory,” “Sports Night,” “Inventing Anna,” and “American Horror Story.” On stage, he’s appeared on Broadway in “Leopoldstadt” and “A Few Good Men,” and in films including “The American President” and “A Few Good Men.”

    as Rabbi Dani

    Rachelle Lefevre

    Rachelle Lefevre rose to fame playing the villainous vampire Victoria in the Twilight film saga and starred as Julia Shumway in CBS’s hit series “Under the Dome.” In “Ethan Bloom,” she plays Rabbi Dani, an intelligent and soulful spiritual mentor with a disarmingly goofy side. She’s worked alongside prestigious actors including Paul Giamatti, Channing Tatum, and John Cusack throughout her 25+ year career.

    as Father Diaz

    Carlos Ponce

    Carlos Ponce is a multi-award-winning actor, singer, and songwriter from Puerto Rico who achieved Double Platinum status with his 1998 debut album. He’s showcased his versatility in projects ranging from “Murder Mystery 2” with Adam Sandler and Jennifer Aniston to “Julie & The Phantoms” and “Couples Retreat.” He holds a Guinness World Record for voicing every character in the Spanish-language Harry Potter Audible saga on Amazon Prime.

    as Elaine Bloomfield

    Mindy Sterling

    Mindy Sterling is best known for her iconic portrayal of Frau Farbissina, Dr. Evil’s German henchwoman, in all three Austin Powers films. She’s a two-time Emmy nominee (2017) for Best Actress in Short Form Comedy or Drama for her work on “Con Man” and “Secs and Execs.” Her extensive voice-over career includes “Legend of Korra,” “The Great North,” “American Dad,” and “Family Guy.”

    as Ethan Bloomfield

    Hank Greenspan

    Hank Greenspan stars as the title character Ethan Bloomfield in this coming-of-age comedy marking his first film role. He’s best known for playing Grover on CBS’s primetime sitcom “The Neighborhood” for eight seasons alongside Cedric the Entertainer, Max Greenfield, and Beth Behrs. He also had a recurring role on Netflix’s “13 Reasons Why” as the young version of Justin Foley.

    as Camille Garcia

    Caroline Valencia

    Caroline Valencia is best known for her role as Young Mabel (young Selena Gomez) in season 2 of the hit Hulu series “Only Murders in the Building.” She landed her first lead role as Elena in the award-winning film “Land of Gold” which premiered at Tribeca Film Festival 2022. She can also be seen in Disney’s “The Slumber Party” and as a guest star on “Wizards Beyond Waverly Place.”

    Behind the camera

    Filmmaking Team

    Director & Producer

    Herschel Faber

    Herschel wrote, directed, and produced Cavemen, a feature-length romantic comedy starring Skylar Astin (Pitch Perfect), Camilla Belle, Chad Michael Murray, and Jason Patric, which was released theatrically by Well Go USA in partnership with Warner Brothers. As a screenwriter, he wrote the cult hit Mac and Devin Go to High School starring Snoop Dogg and Wiz Khalifa, and has placed in the semi-finals of the prestigious Nicholl Fellowship and Austin Film Festival.

    Writer & Producer

    Maylen Dominguez

    Maylen previously worked in development with Minnie Driver at Two Drivers Productions and helped produce content for Latin American television and films at Galan Entertainment, including The ALMA Awards, Los Beltran, and Padre Alberto. As a screenwriter, she won the prestigious Zaki Gordon Award for Screenwriting and an NBC/Universal award for TV writing.

    Director of Photography

    Darmon Moore

    Darmon began his cinematography career in Australia, shooting commercials for major brands like Visa, Emirates, Oreo, and BMX across Australia and Dubai before joining the Australian Cinematographers Society. After relocating to Los Angeles and then Florida, he shot music videos, award-winning short films, the Netflix series The Ultimatum, and the feature film Ethan Bloom.

    Editor

    Robert Schafer

    Robert began his career at American Zoetrope editing featurettes for Francis Ford Coppola films and working for industry legends like Coppola and Walter Murch. As senior editor at Zoetrope, he has edited feature films including Coppola’s Megalopolis, Roman Coppola’s A Glimpse Inside the Mind of Charles Swan III, and Herschel Faber’s award-winning Cavemen.

    Producer

    Joseph Fogel

    Joseph Fogel is a graduate of the University of Chicago and UC Hastings College of Law with extensive experience in independent film and entertainment law, having produced Cavemen and Ethan Bloom. He has been actively involved with “The Filmmakers Alliance” and possesses a deep understanding of copyright law and entertainment representation.

    Producer / Music Supervisor

    Jody Friedman

    Jody brings 25 years in the entertainment business and 53 film and television credits to every film he produces. His first feature as producer, Ethan Bloom, won Best Feature (Comedy) at Sedona, screened at 26+ festivals and opened theatrically through Menemsha Films. He is Music Supervisor for ABC’s The Bachelor franchise and has worked on projects such as Won’t You Be My Neighbor? (Focus Features), Barry, Griselda and Outer Banks.

    Producer

    Michael Elsner

    Michael Elsner brings over 25 years of entertainment industry experience to Ethan Bloom. His work has been featured in Disney’s Cinderella, Star Wars: The Mandalorian, American Idol, and ad campaigns for Audi and Verizon. With over 3,000 placements of original music across tv and film, he possesses a deep understanding of commercial viability in the entertainment marketplace.
