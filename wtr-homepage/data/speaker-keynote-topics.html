Ideal For
Employees, teams, leaders, and mixed corporate audiences
The Talk
Every organization wants higher performance, stronger culture, and leaders who take ownership — yet most overlook the internal patterns quietly shaping all three. Teams don't rise to the level of ambition alone. They rise — or stall — at the level of the patterns they repeatedly operate from. Too often, those patterns were built for past pressure, old incentives, or outdated ways of working — and now they're limiting what's possible.

In this keynote, Jody Friedman introduces the Wreck the Room™ framework — a psychologically grounded methodology that shows why real transformation doesn't come from motivation, mindset hacks, or more information. It comes from dismantling outdated patterns and rebuilding with intention. Through raw personal storytelling, behavioral science, and real-world examples drawn from two decades of high-stakes creative and business leadership — including work with Netflix, HBO, Disney, Marvel, Sony, and Warner Bros. — Jody reveals why many high achievers feel stuck despite success: performing their way through a life that no longer fits who they've become.
Learning Objectives
  • Identify the psychological and identity-based "rooms" — the unconscious patterns, beliefs, and roles — that quietly cap performance, leadership, and fulfillment
  • Recognize the difference between renovating an old identity and truly wrecking it — and why most change efforts fail because they never address the root
  • Apply the Wreck the Room™ three-step framework — Wreck-ognize It, Wreck It, Wrock It — to disrupt cycles, dismantle limiting roles, and rebuild from alignment rather than survival
  • Use practical tools to move from performance mode, stagnation, or burnout into clarity, ownership, and intentional forward momentum

The Talk
