About Jody

Hi, I’m Jody Friedman.

Former CNN & Good Morning America team member, Music Supervisor for The Bachelor franchise, film producer, and multi-7-figure entrepreneur who’s worked with Netflix, HBO, Sony, Warner Bros., Marvel, and Disney.

For over two decades, I’ve helped major studios tell better stories under pressure, from producing feature films to managing million-dollar creative projects. Now I help organizations rewrite theirs.

I help high-achievers, leaders and teams break through the barriers that keep them stuck at the same level. I teach audiences to identify what’s limiting their growth, eliminate those obstacles, and rebuild with clarity and purpose.

What does “Wreck The Room™” mean?

As life happens, we build walls, each brick representing something good or bad that shaped us. Over time, those walls become unconscious rooms we operate from, and eventually, they can become prisons. “Wrecking the room” means identifying these limiting spaces, tearing down what no longer serves you and stepping into the room you’re meant to be in.

Every organization wants higher performance, stronger culture, and leaders who take ownership — yet most overlook the internal patterns quietly shaping all three.
