The Problem

Every production has seen this movie.

01

Temp Love

The director's married to the temp track, and the licensing quote just came back at three times your music budget. Temp with music you can actually keep.

02

The Deadline

The cut changed. The mix is Friday. Air date doesn't move. You need real options today, not after another week of digging.

03

The Clearance

The perfect song dies at the last minute over split ownership, a slow publisher or a surprise fee, after the cut is locked.

04

Library Roulette

Dozens of libraries, millions of tracks, and nothing that fits. Period-authentic vintage, a regional folk recording, the exact door creak: most libraries just don't have it.

The Fix

I've sat in both chairs.

For more than twenty years I've worked as a sync agent pitching music, and for more than twenty years as a music supervisor searching for it. From both sides I kept seeing the same problem: supervisors buried in music that doesn't fit, can't clear in time, or simply doesn't exist in the libraries they use.

HD Music Now is my answer. Curated catalogs, hard-to-find specialty music, half a million sound effects, and a supervisor's ear on every request, so you get what the scene needs and get back to the edit.

I'm also a songwriter and producer. Songs I've written and produced have landed in Law & Order: Organized Crime, Murdaugh: Death in the Family, CBS's Evil and Hulu's The Handmaid's Tale trailer campaign.
