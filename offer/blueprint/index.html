01 · The room you're in

Somebody else drew the floor plan.

Rooms get built early. Childhood adds a wall. Survival adds another. Fear, culture, approval and performance finish the job. By the time you're running a business, you're living inside a structure you never signed off on, and it feels like your personality.

It keeps you adjusting the furniture when the structure is the problem.

The force behind it has a name: inherited design. It doesn't announce itself. It shows up as

  • ConformityDoing it the way it's done because that's how it's done.
  • PerformanceBeing the version of you that gets approved.
  • SilenceNot saying the true thing, for years.
  • ComfortPicking the room you know over the one you want.
  • SmallnessCapping yourself before anyone else has to.

Four rooms. You probably already know which one you're standing in.
