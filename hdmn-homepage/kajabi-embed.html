The Fix

I've sat in both chairs.

For more than twenty years I've worked as a sync agent pitching music, and for more than twenty years as a music supervisor searching for it. From both sides I kept seeing the same problem: supervisors buried in music that doesn't fit, can't clear in time, or simply doesn't exist in the libraries they use.

HD Music Now is my answer. Curated catalogs, hard-to-find specialty music, half a million sound effects, and a supervisor's ear on every request, so you get what the scene needs and get back to the edit.
