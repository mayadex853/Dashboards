FAQ

Questions, answered honestly.

No fine print. If something's not clear after this, just ask us directly.

What exactly is included in the monthly rental?

A fully custom-designed site built around your business, secure hosting with uptime monitoring, SSL (the padlock/https your customers expect), and reasonable ongoing content updates (hours, pricing, photos, a new service you add, small copy changes). Think of it as design, hosting, and a standing "need something changed" line, all rolled into one flat fee.

What's not included?

Large-scale rebuilds (turning a one-page site into a full e-commerce store, for example), stock photography licensing if you want specific paid images, and we don't offer SEO, ads, or lead-generation services. If a request is outside normal updates, we'll tell you plainly rather than quietly charging extra.

How fast do updates happen?

Most small content updates are turned around within a couple of business days. Bigger change requests take a bit longer depending on scope, we'll always give you a timeframe upfront.

Why would I rent instead of just paying for a site once?

Lower upfront cost, and your site is actively maintained for you going forward. If a plugin breaks, your hosting lapses, or you need a small content update, that's on us, not something you have to find a developer for every time it comes up.

What happens if I cancel?

The rented site comes down once your subscription ends, the same way canceling any other software subscription works. If you buy the full buyout option instead, you own the code and the site forever, with nothing to cancel.

Do I own my domain name?

Yes, always. You register and own your own domain (like yourbusiness.com) through whatever registrar you choose. We simply point it at your site. Your domain is never tied to us.

Will this get me more customers or leads?

We're straightforward about this: we don't promise more traffic, leads, or marketing results. This is about giving you a website that looks as professional and credible as your actual business is, so you stop losing trust before someone even calls you.

How long does it take?

You'll typically see a real first draft within a few business days of getting started. From there, revisions happen quickly based on your feedback.

Can I make changes myself?

Under the rental plan, we handle updates for you. The easiest way to request one: record a quick screen share with your voice (we recommend the free tool Loom) walking through exactly what you want changed, and send us the link. It takes less time than writing an email and we get it right the first time more often. Typing it up works too, whatever's easiest for you. If you choose the full buyout, the site and code are entirely yours to edit however you like.

Is there a contract?

No. The rental plan is month-to-month. Cancel anytime, no penalty, no multi-year lock-in.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Mostly local service-based businesses: dental and medical practices, law firms, contractors, HVAC companies, veterinary clinics, chiropractors, boutique fitness studios, architects, and interior designers. If that's not quite you, reach out anyway, we can tell you honestly if it's a fit.
