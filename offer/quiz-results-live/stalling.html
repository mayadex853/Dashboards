02 · the open loop

Welcome To The Comfort Of Almost

Almost is a very specific place. It’s close enough to feel like you’re moving, but not close enough that anything actually changes. You research the decision thoroughly. You talk about it with people you trust. You make the plan, revise the plan, refine the plan. Almost feels productive. That’s what makes it so powerful.

The identity you built needs Almost to exist because Almost means you’re still in control. You haven’t failed yet. You haven’t been exposed. But safe is slowly costing you everything you actually want.

Awareness without execution is just self-torture. You cannot analyze your way out of a room you are frozen in. You have to locate the load-bearing walls and make a real move.
