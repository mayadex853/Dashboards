What We Offer

Comprehensive care, delivered gently

From routine restorations to a smile you're proud to show, every treatment is explained clearly and delivered at a pace that feels right for you.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

Durable, natural-looking composite fillings matched exactly to your tooth color — no silver, no shortcuts.

Inlays & Onlays

A conservative, lab-crafted alternative to a full crown when a filling alone isn't quite enough.

Crowns & Bridges

Protect a weakened tooth or replace a missing one with a restoration built to last for years.

Porcelain Veneers

Mask stains, chips, and misalignment with ultra-thin veneers that look completely natural.

Dental Implants

A permanent, confident replacement for a missing tooth that functions and feels like your own.

Zoom Whitening

Noticeably brighter in a single visit — a simple way to feel good about your smile again.

NTI Night Guards

Custom-fit protection for clenching and grinding, so you wake up without the jaw pain.

Meet Your Dentist

Dr. Andrew Dinh

Dr. Dinh's interest in dentistry started in his teens, volunteering at a local dental office — long before he earned his undergraduate degree from the University of Toronto and his dental degree from Boston University.

That early experience shaped how he practices today: every patient gets individualized care, a clear explanation of their options, and a pace that respects how they actually feel in the chair — not just what the treatment plan calls for.

A Practice Built On Partnership

Dr. Dinh practices alongside Dr. Janice Say

Dr. Andrew Dinh and Dr. Janice Say understand that the relationship between a patient and their dentist is a special one. Together, they work to build long-term partnerships with every patient that result in healthy, confident smiles — always seeking out the latest technology, materials, and techniques to bring into the practice.

Outside the office, Dr. Dinh enjoys spending time with his own family and friends, and unwinding with a round of golf or a day of fishing.
