How It Works

From first conversation to live site, simply.

No technical knowledge needed. We handle the design, the hosting, and the upkeep. You run your business.

1

Tell us about your business

We start with a short conversation (phone, video, or email) about your business, your customers, and what's not working with your current site. If you have a logo, brand colors, or photos, great. If not, we'll work with what you have.

2

We design your site

We build a fully custom site around your business: your services, your story, your real photos. You'll typically see a real first draft within a few business days.

3

Review and refine

Tell us what you'd like changed. The easiest way: record a quick screen share with your voice (we recommend the free tool Loom) walking through the site, or just type it up. We revise until you're happy.

4

Go live

Once you approve it, we point your domain at the new site. You keep full ownership of your domain name. Hosting, security, and SSL are all set up and handled by us.

5

Ongoing updates

Need new hours, prices, photos, or a new service added? Send us a note or a quick recording and we'll take care of it, usually within a couple of business days.

What you can expect from us

Straight answers

If a request is outside normal updates, we'll tell you plainly upfront rather than quietly charging extra.

No lock-in

Month-to-month. Cancel anytime, or buy the site outright if you'd rather own it.

Honest positioning

We don't promise leads or rankings. We promise a site that represents your business as well as you do.
