What it means to “Wreck The Room™”

As life happens, we build walls.

Each brick represents an experience, expectation, win, or wound that shaped how we learned to operate. Over time, those walls become the rooms you live and lead from.

At first, those rooms help us survive and succeed. They create safety, structure, and momentum.

Eventually, they become invisible. And what once protected you starts limiting you.

Wrecking the room means consciously identifying what no longer serves you, dismantling the structures you’ve outgrown, and intentionally choosing how you operate next, before the room decides for you.
