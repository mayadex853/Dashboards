First Time Here?

Here's exactly what to expect

No surprises, no scripts. Just a clear look at what happens from the moment you walk in.

1

Have an emergency? That comes first.

If you're coming in with an urgent problem, we address that right away and keep you comfortable before anything else.

2

A thorough first exam — about 90 minutes

Without an emergency, your first visit is a comprehensive exam, diagnosis, and cleaning. We'll talk through your concerns, review your medical and dental history, take any x-rays we need, check your soft tissues with an oral cancer screening, evaluate your gums, and examine each tooth for decay or restorations that need attention — then clean your teeth.

3

A plan, not a pitch

Based on what we find and when you last had a cleaning, we may recommend further preventive care. Straightforward treatment can usually be discussed right there at the same visit. If your case is more involved, we'll set up a separate consultation so there's time to go through it properly.

Forms to fill out beforehand

  • Acknowledgement of Patient Responsibility
  • HIPAA Notice
  • HIPAA Authorization

We'll send these to you ahead of your appointment so check-in is quick.

Ready to get on the schedule?

Call us directly or send a request and we'll follow up to find a time that works.
