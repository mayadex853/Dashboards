The Talk
Every entrepreneur in your audience is navigating AI right now. The tools are evolving fast. The pressure to adapt is real. But the founders who will win aren't the ones with access to the best tools - they're the ones with the strongest internal operating system running underneath them.

In this keynote, Jody reveals why AI disruption is exposing something most entrepreneurs have never addressed - the outdated identity patterns quietly creating resistance, fear, and stagnation in the face of rapid change. Through behavioral science and two decades of high-stakes creative leadership, attendees walk away with a clear framework to strengthen the human skills no algorithm can replicate and lead with clarity in a world that isn't slowing down.

Your audience won't just understand AI better. They'll understand themselves better - and that's the real competitive advantage.
Learning Objectives
  • Understand why AI disruption is exposing outdated internal patterns - and why the founders who adapt fastest have the strongest internal operating system
  • Identify the resistance, fear, or stagnation showing up in their business as a result of rapid technological change
  • Apply the Wreck the Room™ framework to strengthen adaptability, ownership, and human performance in AI-driven environments
  • Build the mindset and internal capacity required to lead, decide, and thrive when everything around them keeps changing
