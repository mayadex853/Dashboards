Behind the Chair

Modern equipment, less guesswork

Every treatment room is fully computerized, so what we find, what we recommend, and what we do about it all happens with less radiation, less noise, and fewer surprises.

Digital X-Rays

A small sensor placed in the mouth sends an image straight to a computer screen — almost instantly, with up to 90% less radiation than traditional film x-rays.

Diagnodent Laser

Used during exams, a harmless laser light scans each tooth's structure and can detect decay starting below the surface, often before it would show up any other way.

Computerized Treatment Rooms

Every treatment room is fully computerized, with digital cameras and x-rays feeding directly into diagnosis and treatment planning.

Networked Front Desk

Workstations throughout the office are linked to the front desk, so appointment scheduling, billing, and insurance information stay in sync without extra back-and-forth.

Digital Cameras & Imaging

Intraoral cameras and computer imaging let us show you exactly what we're seeing — so a recommendation is never just taken on faith.

Hospital-Grade Sterilization

A steam sterilizer is checked weekly with spore testing and inspected daily, backed up by ultrasound and cold sterilization. Every switch, button, and instrument panel is covered in plastic film and changed between patients, and water lines run on self-contained reservoirs supplied with purified, bottled distilled water.
