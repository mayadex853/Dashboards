# Council Application Call: Calendly questions

**Event:** Council Application Call (Council), 45 min, Zoom
**Link:** https://calendly.com/jody_friedman/council-application-call-council

**Where to add them:** Calendly → event type → Booking page options → Invitee questions. Delete the default "Please share anything…" question first.

Each question either prequalifies the applicant or gets them to answer an objection themselves before the call.

---

**1. What best describes your current role?** *(One answer, required)*
- Business owner / founder
- Executive / C-suite leader
- Coach / consultant
- Other

**2. What's your current annual revenue or income?** *(One answer, required)*
- $250K to $500K
- $500K to $1M
- $1M to $5M
- $5M+
- Prefer not to say

**3. What have you already tried to break this pattern (coaches, therapy, retreats, programs), and why do you think it didn't stick?** *(Paragraph, required)*
> Gets them to say, in their own words, that surface-level fixes haven't worked. This handles the "I've tried this before" objection.

**4. What is staying where you are costing you right now? Think time, money and the people closest to you.** *(Paragraph, required)*
> Gets them to put a price on doing nothing before they hear yours.

**5. It's 12 months from now and we're on a call. You're telling me what shifted. What's different?** *(Paragraph, required)*

**6. Council runs for 14 weeks, with 7 live group calls and 3 private sessions with Jody. Can you commit to showing up to every call and doing the work in between?** *(One answer, required)*
- Yes, I'm in
- Yes, I'll make room for it
- I'm not sure I have the time right now
> Handles the time objection. Only applicants who answer "Yes" qualify for the Do-the-Work Guarantee.

**7. Council works because a small group of 6 to 8 founders and leaders does the deep work together. How do you feel about that?** *(One answer, required)*
- That's exactly what I want
- I'm open to it
- I'd rather work one on one
> "One on one" means you pitch Inner Circle on the call.

**8. Council is $15,000, or 2 monthly payments of $8,000. If we both agree it's the right fit, are you in a position to invest?** *(One answer, required)*
- Yes, I'm ready to pay in full
- Yes, with the payment plan
- Not right now
> Handles price before the call, so you don't spend 45 minutes with someone who can't buy.

**9. Will everyone who needs to be part of this decision be on the call (spouse, partner, business partner)?** *(One answer, required)*
- Yes
- No, I make this decision on my own
- No, but I'll talk with them before the call

**10. Why is now the right time, and not six months from now?** *(Paragraph, required)*
> Gets them to give their own reason not to wait.

**11. Anything else you want Jody to know before the call?** *(Paragraph, optional)*

---

**Optional line to add to the event description:**
> Council includes the Do-the-Work Guarantee: show up to every call and complete every lesson, and if you don't see any change, you join the next Council free.

**Also recommended:** in Calendly, set this event to **Secret** so it only shows up from the sales page, not on your public Calendly profile.
